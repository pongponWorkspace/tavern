Tavern design system

Tokens and components for Tavern, the project knowledge board. Dense, accessible, built for scanning many links quickly. One electric cobalt accent for action, semantic color for state, three fixed hues for item types.

Version 1.0 · 14 Oct 2026 Target: WCAG 2.2 AA People, projects and files on this page are example data

Brand

The mark is a pushpin seen from the side, which reads as a T. Pinning is how teams mark the sources that matter, and Tavern is the board they pin to. It always sits on a cobalt plate and never gets effects, outlines or gradients.

Name

Write Tavern in running text. Use “Project Knowledge Board” only as the descriptor under the wordmark or on the sign-in page. Never “TAVERN” or “Tavern App”.

Clear space

Keep space equal to a quarter of the mark's width on every side. Minimum mark size is 16px; the descriptor needs 24px marks or larger. One-color use drops the plate and draws the pin in the text color.

Wordmark

Public Sans Bold, tracking −1.5%. The gap between mark and wordmark is 40% of the mark's width.

Voice

Plain, specific and calm. Name the action, say what happens to access, never hint at hidden things.

DoArchive itemButtons name the exact action.
Don'tSubmitGeneric verbs hide the consequence.
DoArthit will lose access to Atlas Mobile App right away.State the access change and when it happens.
Don'tAre you sure? This action cannot be undone!Alarm without information.
DoThis page isn't available. It may have moved, or you may not have access.Same message whether the item exists or not.
Don'tYou don't have permission to view “Q3 pricing model”.Leaks the name of a restricted item.

Color

Cool neutrals carry the interface. Electric cobalt means interactive or selected and nothing else. It sits between classic link blue and indigo, saturated enough to be recognizably Tavern. Green, amber and red mean state. Item types own three fixed hues so a link, a file and a document are told apart before anyone reads the title.

Use sentence case everywhere, including buttons, tabs and table headers. Use tabular numerals in tables, counters and dates. Keep running text under 70 characters per line.

Space, shape, motion

A 4px grid. Tight inside a group, generous between groups. Borders separate; shadows only lift things that float above the page.

Control heights

Small in dense tables and toolbars. Large only on sign-in and empty states. Touch targets on mobile get at least 40px of hit area.

Elevation

FlatPanels, cards, tables. Border only.
Raised · shadow-mdMenus, popovers, hovered cards.
Overlay · shadow-lgDialogs, toasts, command palette.

Motion

Fast 100ms for hover and color. Base 160ms for toggles. Slow 240ms with ease-out for things that appear: menus, dialogs, toasts. Nothing animates on page load. Reduced motion turns all of it off.

Icons

Lucide, 1.75 stroke. 16px in controls and text, 14px in metadata, 20px in empty states. Icons support a label; icon-only buttons always carry an accessible name and a tooltip.

Buttons

One primary button per view. Secondary for everything else. Danger buttons stay quiet until hovered, and destructive actions go through a confirm dialog.
